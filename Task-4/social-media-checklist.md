Social Media Do’s 

1. Update privacy settings
Regularly review your account settings to control who can see your posts and personal information.


2. Fact-check before sharing
Always verify the authenticity of information to avoid spreading false or misleading content.


3. Use professional headshots
A clear and appropriate profile picture helps create a positive and professional online image.


4. Engage with industry leaders
Following and interacting with experts can help you learn, network, and stay updated in your field.


5. Showcase your projects
Sharing your work or achievements highlights your skills and can attract academic or career opportunities.




---

Social Media Don’ts 

1. Don’t post sensitive personal data
Avoid sharing private details like phone numbers, addresses, or important documents.


2. Don’t engage in “comment wars”
Online arguments can harm your reputation; it’s better to stay respectful or disengage.


3. Don’t tag people in unprofessional photos
This may affect both your image and others’ comfort or reputation.


4. Avoid oversharing location data
Posting your real-time location can compromise your safety and privacy.


5. Never share login credentials
Keeping passwords and login details private is essential to protect your accounts from misuse.
