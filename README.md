
Project Overview

Title: Digital Literacy Portfolio: Navigating the Modern Professional Landscape
Objective: To establish a secure, professional, and collaborative digital presence while developing the skills necessary to communicate effectively and safely in an academic and corporate environment.
Scope: This project covers five key domains: visual communication, professional networking, technical platforms, workplace etiquette, and cybersecurity awareness.

Task-Wise Content

Task 1: Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic

 * Key Points:
   * Stay Secure: Use 12+ character passwords and hardware/app-based MFA.
   * Digital Footprint: Your "Online Self" is your permanent resume; curate it wisely.
   * Netiquette: Be professional in forums; avoid "flaming" or informal slang in academic groups.
 * Design Tip: Use a "Traffic Light" color scheme (Red for dangers, Green for best practices).

Task 3: Coding & Collaboration Platforms

 * HackerRank Goal: Complete the "Python: If-Else" or "Java: Stdin and Stdout" challenge.
 * Google Form Quiz (5 Questions):
   * What does MFA stand for? (Multiple Choice)
   * Is it safe to use the same password for all accounts? (True/False)
   * Which platform is best for professional networking? (Multiple Choice)
   * Define 'Phishing' in your own words. (Short Answer)
   * How often should you update your software? (Multiple Choice)
     
Task 4: Professional Email & Etiquette Guide

 * Email 1 (Extension): Subject: Extension Request: CSE0001 Assignment | [Name] | [Reg No]. Focus on a valid reason and a proposed new deadline.
 * Social Media Do's:
   * Update privacy settings.
   * Fact-check before sharing.
   * Use professional headshots.
   * Engage with industry leaders.
   * Showcase your projects.
 * Social Media Don'ts:
   * Don't post sensitive personal data.
   * Don't engage in "comment wars."
   * Don't tag people in unprofessional photos.
   * Avoid oversharing location data.
   * Never share login credentials.
     
Task 5: Cybercrime Case Study (UPI Fraud)

 * Scenario: A student receives a "Collect Request" on a UPI app disguised as a "Refund."
 * Step-by-Step:
   * Attacker sends a QR code or payment link.
   * Attacker claims "Enter PIN to receive money."
   * Victim enters PIN.
   * Money is debited instantly.
 * Prevention: Never enter a UPI PIN to receive money. Check the "Verified Merchant" badge.
